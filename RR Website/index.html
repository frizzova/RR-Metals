About RR Metals

Built on Steel.
Driven by Trust.

RR Metals & Form Work is Hyderabad's trusted name in Aluminium Mivan formwork solutions. Over the past 2.5 years, we have built an unshakeable reputation across Telangana's construction industry — delivering quality, reliability, and the most competitive prices in the market.

We specialize in Aluminium Mivan Formwork — the modern construction technology used by Hyderabad's top developers. Whether you need to rent, purchase, or sell formwork systems, RR Metals is your single trusted partner.

Our network spans from Miyapur to Mumbai, Narsingi to Bangalore, and Chennai — a testament to the trust builders place in our name. We don't just supply formwork — we power skylines.
